The live November 25 count still has a gap

At 15:49:02 IST, the Dhan audit logged 67,905,584 accepted row submissions and 55,744,893 rows in the two QuestDB tables. The recorded difference was 12,160,691 after three reads. The saved audit receipt reports the same values.

A later read counted 55,755,208 rows. That leaves 12,150,376 against the earlier accepted count. These totals do not identify individual lost market ticks. Replaying an existing database key can also widen the difference.

Which records can you account for?

For the restart check, timestamp, trading date and feed identify an audit receipt. Comparing the expected rows and their fields catches missing or changed receipts. A matching total alone can hide one missing row and one duplicate.

An audit receipt summarizes the application’s counts. Proving that every market tick arrived requires an authoritative list or sequence from the source and a match against stored ticks.

Measurement details and sources

The 41 ns result came from GitHub Actions run 45597513658 at commit 34aa4860c. The two separate restart runs used QuestDB 9.3.5 on a local, disk-backed fixture. They compared 11 fields per receipt, including the verdict and accepted, stored and missing row counts. Each search timed HTTP to a local QuestDB instance. The order-event lookup ran after 40 warm-ups. A separate conservation query returned a seeded mismatch of 2,064 versus 2,060 and a 97-versus-97 match before and after restart. Its p99 was 47.046 ms before restart and 13.214 ms afterward across 300 reads each.
