live websocket — exchange stamp → arrived here, per socket
One row per socket slot the app tracks (16: 5 main-feed, 5 depth-20, 5 depth-200, 1 order-update), read live from the app's own counters — NOT from the database. instruments on wire is what that socket is subscribed to right now; frames delivered is how many data-carrying messages it has produced this session; last data is how long ago the latest one arrived ("never" means it has not delivered once). A socket holding instruments with zero frames is highlighted — that is the "connected but deaf" case. ticks timed is how many ticks had a usable exchange stamp for the lag columns. ±1 second floor: the exchange sends its timestamp in whole seconds, so this shows outages and drift honestly but can never show milliseconds — treat a sub-second reading as "under a second", not as a precise number. Figures are bucket upper bounds, so they over-state slightly within a bucket. "+Inf" means slower than the largest bucket, i.e. unbounded. An empty table means the live feed produced no ticks at all — never read that as "fast".
